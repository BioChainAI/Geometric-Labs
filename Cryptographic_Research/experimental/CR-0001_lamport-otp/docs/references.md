# References

## Scheme

* L. Lamport, "Password authentication with insecure communication," *Communications of the ACM* 24(11):770–772, 1981.
* N. Haller, "The S/KEY One-Time Password System," RFC 1760, 1995.
* N. Haller, C. Metz, P. Nesser, M. Straw, "A One-Time Password System," RFC 2289, 1998. The race attack is in §9.0.
* M. Jakobsson, "Fractal hash sequence representation and traversal," IEEE ISIT 2002.
* D. Coppersmith, M. Jakobsson, "Almost optimal hash sequence traversal," Financial Cryptography 2002.

## Geometric and quaternion hashing

* J. H. Conway, D. A. Smith, *On Quaternions and Octonions*, A K Peters, 2003. Chapter 5 covers factorization in the Lipschitz and Hurwitz orders.
* A. Lubotzky, R. Phillips, P. Sarnak, "Ramanujan graphs," *Combinatorica* 8(3):261–277, 1988.
* J.-P. Tillich, G. Zémor, "Hashing with SL2," CRYPTO 1994.
* D. X. Charles, E. Z. Goren, K. E. Lauter, "Cryptographic hash functions from expander graphs," *Journal of Cryptology* 22(1):93–113, 2009.
* J.-P. Tillich, G. Zémor, "Collisions for the LPS expander graph hash function," EUROCRYPT 2008.
* C. Petit, K. Lauter, J.-J. Quisquater, "Full cryptanalysis of LPS and Morgenstern hash functions," SCN 2008.
