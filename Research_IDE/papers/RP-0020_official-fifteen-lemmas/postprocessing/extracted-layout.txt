       Abstract
       This monograph gives complete proofs of the ﬁfteen lemmas on which the Symbolic High Dimensional
       Context Compression Protocol (SHD-CCP), in its 64-bit cubic form, and the Geometric Engine of Time rest,
       and derives from them how many engines behave together. Lemmas 1–3 are Zhao's three lemmas in
       geometry (incircle–excircle homothety, centres of spiral similarity and the symmedian harmonic bundle),
       proved in full as the synthetic and projective foundation. Lemmas 4, 5, 10 and 11 ﬁx the engine geometry:
       the tier laws Rn = 5n² + 15n + 15, rn = 2n + 4 and Wn = 16 − 2n with WΣ = 60, the order-6 Hurwitz spinor
       unit h = (1 − i + j + k)/2, the harmonic cubit lengths, ﬁve disjoint spherical shells separated from the voxel
       cube by (11 − 2√3) C₀ ≈ 4.05 m, and the dichotomy between rational clock closure and golden ergodic
       subdivision. Lemmas 6, 7, 8, 12 and 14 govern the dynamics: ﬁve-moment Vandermonde conservation
       across the gap, the 9 + 3 = 12 aﬃne frame and the implied fourth H₄ = (H₁H₂H₃)⁻¹ as a parity element, the
       eight-step cycle with its Step 6 projection into hyperbolic space through the {4,3,5} honeycomb, exact
       holonomy telescoping, and the Fokker–Planck circularity boundary. Lemmas 9, 13 and 15 settle arithmetic
       and integrity: closed-form Garner inverses for the prime triplet (881, 883, 887), Niven's ﬁnite-order bound
       with linear bit growth, and recursive Merkle integrity as against the South Halo pre-ﬁlter. Section 6 speciﬁes
       the 64-bit Einstein Tile, whose Form ID selects exact, lossy or codex readings of the payload. Section 7
       derives, with no new lemma, the 3:1 face-centred-cubic interleave, the handshake windows at Steps 2 and
       6, interaction without contact, twelve-engine parity memory, the collapse of three ghost tetrahedra into the
       central cube, and the four-structure system with its 64 logged streams. Ten scripts reproduce every
       computation, and all pass.

 Rule for mixed claims. Where two tags are joined by a slash, the statement has both characters. [ESTABLISHED /
 PROVEN] marks a standard result that is nonetheless proved in full here (Lemmas 1–3 and 11); [PROVEN /

 evaluates is proven.

 What this monograph does not claim.

        It does not claim a structure-preserving map between the 64 voxels and the 64 rigid isotopy classes of real plane
        sextics, or between any other pair of equal counts; such matches are tagged [CORRESPONDENCE] (Lemma 5 Part
        6, §7.7).
        It does not claim that any map from an elliptic-curve group into the 64-bit tile inverts the discrete logarithm in O(1);
        Lemma 9 Part 4 proves three obstructions.
        It does not treat the 8-bit South Halo as an integrity check. The South Halo is a cache pre-ﬁlter, and integrity rests
        on the recursive Merkle root (Lemma 15).
        It does not claim that the Fokker–Planck sieve computes primality. The sieve samples a prime set it is given (Lemma
        14 Part 4).
        It does not rely on the blowup claim of OpenAI (2026), whose overall correctness is publicly disputed; only the
        results re-proved here are used (Lemma 4 Part 6′, Lemma 6).
        It does not claim that the hierarchies of Section 7 store more than the bits written. Their gains are erasure
        tolerance, phase throughput and a conserved invariant (§7.4, §7.6).
        It does not settle the open problems P1–P16, which are collected in the companion paper.

        3. Each component of a quaternion product AB is a signed sum of 4 pairwise products of components of A
           and B, so ‖AB‖∞ ≤ 4‖A‖∞‖B‖∞. Induction over k steps gives ‖Pk‖∞ < 4k−1(2b)k = 2k(b+2)−2.
        4. Direct expansion gives (1 + i)² = 1 + 2i − 1 = 2i, (1 + i)⁴ = (2i)² = −4, and (1 + i)⁸ = (−4)² = +16. For
           any projective quaternion Q = w + v ∈ Z⁴ rotating by angle α = 2θ, ‖v‖² / w² = tan² θ ∈ Q; for α = 45° (θ
          = 22.5°), tan²(22.5°) = (√2 − 1)² = 3 − 2√2 ∉ Q, proving impossibility. ■

      Lemma 15 (Pigeonhole Preimage Bounds, South Halo Cache Pre-Filtering and Recursive Merkle
      Veriﬁcation across Nested Cubic Layers). [PROVEN]

        1. 12-Layer Pigeonhole Preimage Bound ([PROVEN]): For any 64-bit summary label τ: V₆₄¹² → {0, 1}⁶⁴,

          in the deeper layers Gm+1, …, G₁₂.

           bit South Halo SH_ID partitions the 2⁵⁶ possible 56-bit payloads into 2⁸ = 256 buckets of average size:
                                         256
                                             = 248 ≈ 2.81 × 1014 payloads per South Halo ID.
                                         28
          Consequently, the 8-bit South Halo check in the QRE middleware is strictly a fast O(1) L1/L2 cache
          resonance pre-ﬁlter (rejecting non-matching phase/wheel IDs in nanoseconds), and never a
          standalone cryptographic integrity check against adversarial payload modiﬁcation.
        3. Recursive Bottom-Up Merkle Hash Chain ([PROVEN]): Let H: {0,1}∗ → {0,1}κ be a collision-resistant
           cryptographic hash function (e.g., SHA-256 / BLAKE3 with κ = 256 bits stored in the packet attestation

                                τ12 (G) = H(G12 ),   τd (G) = H(Gd ∥ τd+1 (G))   for d = 11, 10, … , 1.

        1. By the Pigeonhole Principle, partitioning 2⁷² states into 2⁶⁴ buckets yields at least one bucket of size ≥

           unchanged.
        2. Partitioning 2⁵⁶ payloads into 2⁸ Halo IDs gives an average ﬁber size of 2⁵⁶ / 2⁸ = 2⁴⁸.

           τd∗+1(G′)) are distinct while their hash outputs under H are equal, exhibiting an explicit collision of H. ■

 7.1 Tessellation and the 3:1 Interleave

      Theorem 7.2 (3:1 Interleave and the Implied Fourth). [PROVEN]
      Let L = 2Z³ and o₀ = (0,0,0), o₁ = (1,1,0), o₂ = (1,0,1), o₃ = (0,1,1). Then:

        3. o₁ + o₂ + o₃ ≡ 0 (mod 2): the oﬀsets form the Klein four-group, so any three of the four translates

        4. The points 2oi − (1,1,1) are the vertices of a regular tetrahedron inscribed in the cube (the 0° tetrahedron

 7.7 The 64 Streams
