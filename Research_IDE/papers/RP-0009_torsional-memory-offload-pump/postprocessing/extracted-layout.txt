                                                  Abstract
       We formalize a three-scale quaternionic Markov pump whose rotational memory is distributed
       across three coupled cyclic gears: an outer high-resolution memory layer (12-point gear, 720-
       frame chain), a middle inverse-compensating layer (10-point gear, 72-frame chain), and an inner
       coarse-equilibrium layer (8-point gear, 10-frame chain). Each layer stores unit-quaternion incre-
       ments (deltas); coarse layers are obtained from fine layers by ordered products of consecutive
       deltas, and fine detail is reconstructed from coarse layers by fractional-power (SLERP) lifts. We
       show that the block products telescope to exact net rotations, that the variable down-sampling
       blocks partition the frame index, and that the fractional-power lift reconstructs exactly on the
       principal branch. Treating the three gear layers as the states of a Markov chain, a net cyclic
       current J12,10 + J10,8 + J8,12 > 0 encodes directed (non-reversible) circulation of memory rather
       than passive diffusion; at stationarity the three edge currents coincide, so the pump condition
       reduces to positivity of a single cycle current. Geometric coherence is maintained by closing the
       cycle product H = Q(8) Q(10) Q(12) to the identity up to a controlled holonomy, with residual
       drift redistributed across the gears. We close by describing how this multi-resolution mem-
       ory hierarchy interoperates with the executable, parity-safe Hyperbolic Sparsemax Pump, with
       which it shares both the unit-quaternion state space and the broken-detailed-balance current
       condition.


1     Introduction
The system is a three-scale quaternionic Markov pump whose memory is distributed across three
coupled cyclic gears. Informally,

    large memory chain ←→ compressed inverse correction chain ←→ coarse equilibrium chain,

where information flows both upward and downward through quaternionic delta maps while pre-
serving rotational coherence. The outer chain is the high-resolution memory layer, the middle chain
is the inverse compensating layer, and the inner chain is the coarse torsional anchor. The remainder
of this paper makes each arrow precise: downward flow compresses fine rotation history into coarse
torsional updates, and upward flow lifts coarse rotations back into finer chains through quaternion
interpolation. A three-state Markov current supplies the directed circulation that distinguishes an
active memory pump from passive reversible storage, and a holonomy constraint keeps the whole
mechanism in geometric equilibrium.

14    Interoperability with the Hyperbolic Sparsemax Pump
The companion Hyperbolic Sparsemax Pump [1] is an executable, parity-safe transition kernel on
the 64-voxel SHD–CCP lattice: rational, row-stochastic, exactly sparse, and free of transcendental
operations. The two constructions are complementary rather than redundant, and they share
enough structure to be coupled directly.

A common non-equilibrium primitive. Both pumps are driven by the same broken-detailed-
balance current. The sparsemax pump defines a base-referenced current Jij0 = πi0 K  b ij − π 0 K
                                                                                               b
                                                                                            j ji on
the lattice; the present pump defines Jij = πi Kij − πj Kji on the gear layers. These are the same
primitive at two scales: the sparsemax current circulates orientation within the fine lattice, while
the torsional current (Lemma 5) circulates memory between the three coarse layers. A single design
quantity, the cycle affinity, governs both.

A shared state space. Both layers represent states as unit quaternions. The sparsemax voxel
spinors qi = (wi , mi ) ∈ S 3 ∩ Q4 are exactly the kind of unit quaternion stored by the outer chain
    (12)
as qn . The sparsemax kernel can therefore act as the per-frame generator of the outer memory
chain: each lattice transition emits a unit-quaternion increment, and the offload pump compresses
the resulting 720-frame history downward and reconstructs it upward on demand.

A clean rational/transcendental split. The sparsemax forward path is deliberately exact-
rational, with no exp, log, or root, so that the discrete parity invariant is bit-reproducible. The
offload pump’s upward reconstruction, by contrast, uses fractional powers q w = exp(w log q), and
its holonomy correction uses ε = log H. This division of labour maps precisely onto the sparsemax
paper’s octet-residual quarantine, which requires that transcendental and irrational quantities never
re-enter the rational kernel: one keeps the rational sparsemax kernel on the executable forward path
and confines the SLERP lifts and holonomy correction to the offline memory-reconstruction layer,
where such residuals are already quarantined. Where reconstructed states must re-enter the lattice,
they are rationalized first, exactly as the quarantine prescribes.

A shared torsional index. Both constructions carry a 12-fold torsional structure: the sparse-
max lattice resolves its face-neighbor transitions into 12 torsional coupling channels, and the outer
gear here is a 12-point gear. Identifying the two gives one 12-fold index that the fine kernel scores
and the coarse pump compresses.
    In combination, the sparsemax pump is the rational, parity-safe transition engine at the leaf
scale, and the torsional offload pump is the multi-resolution memory hierarchy that stores, com-
presses, and reconstructs the orientation history that engine produces, both governed by the same
directed-circulation condition. Together they realize a complete context-compression loop for the
SHD–CCP setting that both papers address.


15    Scope and Outlook
This paper specifies a constructive model. The local results are proved: the block products tele-
scope to exact net rotations (Proposition 2), the down-sampling blocks partition the middle index
(Proposition 3), the fractional-power lift reconstructs exactly on the principal branch (Proposi-
tion 4), and the three gear currents coincide at stationarity (Lemma 5). The global claims, that
the pump offloads and reconstructs memory coherently, are an engineering design rather than a
The Torsional Markov Memory-Offload Pump                                                         9


theorem: quantitative reconstruction-error bounds (the fractional lift is exact only on a single
principal-branch increment, not for accumulated drift) and the existence of an explicit transition
matrix K and stationary π realizing a prescribed cycle affinity are natural next steps. The inward-
folding recursion of Section 12 is schematic, as noted there. With these caveats, the mechanism
is, mathematically, a multi-resolution quaternion delta chain with an inverse middle compensator
and a non-equilibrium Markov circulation current, designed to interoperate with the executable
Hyperbolic Sparsemax Pump.
