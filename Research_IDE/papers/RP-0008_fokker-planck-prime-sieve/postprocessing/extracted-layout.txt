         Abstract
         This installment of The Geometric Engine of Time formalizes the Fokker-Planck
         prime sieve on quaternionic lattices and proves what it can and cannot do. On a
         lattice the sieve is a master equation whose Gibbs state is positive on every node;
         absorbing prime traps send all mass to the primes with the harmonic measure,
         and on the infinite Hurwitz lattice the sieve is defined exactly when the Hurwitz
         partition function converges. Drift along lattice lines without a potential cannot
         sieve: by the cycle-current formula of the cubit installment it carries a current and
         produces entropy, yet its stationary state is uniform. The sieve therefore does not
         compute the primes. Under the series' Dual-Helix principle it proposes trap sites
         and exact arithmetic certifies them, and the Engine's 30-node wheel gives the
         proposer a proven bias of 3.75, the largest of any primorial wheel whose unit
         classes fit the packet's 3-bit wheel field. The sieve lives in four dimensions. The
         golden Clifford torus contains no lattice point, while its Fibonacci approximants
         carry the points (F_m, F_{m+1}, F_{m−1}, F_m), whose prime norms beyond 3,
         namely 7, 47 and 2207, are congruent to 7 mod 8 and so missing from every
         three-dimensional lattice. In exact arithmetic the sieve has no drift, and a periodic
         delta schedule returns to the identity within 12 repetitions or never. The
         quaternions 1 ± 2i, 1 ± 2j, 1 ± 2k turn the six steps of the cubic lattice into a free
         group whose words form a 6-regular hyperbolic tree over the cubic lattice; every
         address factors back to its unique word, and reduction modulo a prime gives
         Ramanujan graphs on which the sieve's diffusion mixes in 3.25 time units against
         20.15 on a cubic torus of the same size. On the Einstein Tile the sieve reads the
         Form 1 Hurwitz core in exact 64-bit arithmetic and certifies prime-norm anchor
         packets with a 2,017-byte table. Anchored to the realized cubit, whose uncertainty
         caps the informative quadrance near 2.3 × 10⁷, the Triple Quad Formula stays
         exact in 64-bit integers, where double precision declares a non-collinear lattice
         triangle collinear. Together these define a universal lattice standard on which
         agents with no shared orientation, medium or history align non-locally by
         invariants and locally by Horn's quaternion method, with every alignment certified
         exactly and recorded under a Merkle root. Its use for AI communication, alignment
         and governance is stated as a design proposal; the mathematics beneath it is
         proven, established or computed.

 2.2 Absorbing Traps and the Dual-Helix Sampler
       Proposition 2.3 (Absorbing Traps and Harmonic Measure). [PROVEN]
       Formalizes the selection rule of source §2 and the trap of source §4; also Fifteen
       Lemmas, Lemma 14 Part 2.
       Make every prime-quadrance node absorbing and keep the rates of Definition 2.1
       elsewhere, on a connected finite lattice with at least one prime node. From any start
       the mass converges to a distribution supported only on the prime nodes; its value at
       a prime node a is the probability of first entering the prime set at a, the harmonic
       measure of the prime set. Every transient mode decays at a rate of at least λ_min >
       0.

       Proof. Let L_TT be the generator restricted to the transient nodes and L_TA its rates
       into the traps. Every transient node has a path to a trap, so −L_TT is invertible and
       the transient mass decays at least as e^(−λ_min t). The mass absorbed from node t
       is row t of (−L_TT)⁻¹ L_TA, the hitting distribution of the prime set. ■

 Remark (The 4 × 4 × 4 Lattice). [COMPUTED] With V = 0 and D = 1 the 25 prime nodes
 trap all the mass; the slowest transient mode decays at λ_min = 1.402, the mean absorption
 time from a uniform start is 0.295 in units of 1/D, and the final trap masses range from 0.031
 to 0.045.

 The Dual-Helix report samples candidates with ∂ρ/∂t = ∇·(D(Q)∇ρ) − V(Q)ρ, with D(Q) = 1/(1 +
 αχ(Q)) slowed by the TQF collinearity feature χ (BioChain AI 2026a, equation 23). The term
 −Vρ removes mass at rate V: it is a killing term, a soft trap.

       Proposition 2.4 (Killing Concentrates but Does Not Select). [PROVEN]
       Formalizes BioChain AI (2026a), equation (23), on a lattice.
       Let the killing rate be 0 on prime nodes and κ > 0 on the others. The total mass
       decays, and the normalized surviving mass converges to the left Perron eigenvector

       of the generator minus diag(κ), which is strictly positive on every node of a
       connected lattice.

       Proof. The generator minus a non-negative diagonal has non-negative off-diagonal
       entries and an irreducible pattern, so by Perron–Frobenius its leading eigenvalue is
       real and simple with a strictly positive eigenvector, and every other mode decays
       faster relative to it. ■

 Gibbs barriers reach higher concentration without destroying mass, and only absorbing traps

 2.3 The Infinite Lattice and Drift Without a Potential
 Two results of the Fifteen Lemmas complete the picture: the sieve extends from a finite box to
 the whole Hurwitz lattice exactly when a series converges, and a drift along lattice lines with
 no potential cannot sieve at all.

       Lemmas, Lemma 14 Part 3; the sharpness statement is new.
       On the infinite Hurwitz lattice with a radial potential V(q) = V(N(q)), the Gibbs state of
       Proposition 2.2 exists exactly when the partition function converges:
                                                    ∞

       For V(n) = α ln n this holds exactly when βα > 2, and every linear trap V(n) = κn with
       κ > 0 qualifies.

       Proof. The shell of norm n holds 24σ_odd(n) Hurwitz integers (Proposition 4.1), which
       gives the series. For V = α ln n it is 24 Σ σ_odd(n) n^(−s) with s = βα, and as a
       Dirichlet series Σ σ_odd(n) n^(−s) = (1 − 2^(1−s)) ζ(s) ζ(s − 1). This converges for s

 The sieve belongs in four dimensions: the Hurwitz order carries every prime, a quarter of the

 4.1 The Hurwitz Order and the Three-Square Obstruction
 A Hurwitz quaternion H = a + bi + cj + dk has four integer or four half-integer components; its
 norm N(H) = a² + b² + c² + d² = HH̄ is a non-negative integer. The Hurwitz quaternions form a
 ring with left and right division with remainder, and its 24 units form the binary tetrahedral
 group 2T.

       Proposition 4.1 (No Inert Primes; Norm Counts). [ESTABLISHED] Formalizes
       source §3.
       (1) Every rational prime p is the norm of a Hurwitz integer π, so p = ππ̄ and no prime
       is inert. (2) The number of Hurwitz integers of norm n is 24 times the sum of the odd
       divisors of n; for an odd prime p it is 24(p + 1). (3) A Hurwitz integer is irreducible
       exactly when its norm is prime.

 Part (1) is Lagrange's four-square theorem; parts (2) and (3) are Hurwitz's (Conway and Smith
 2003). An integer quaternion of prime norm is primitive, since g = gcd(a, b, c, d) gives g²
 dividing p [PROVEN]; the note's stability condition is therefore automatic on prime shells.

       Theorem 4.2 (Three-Square Obstruction). [ESTABLISHED] Gives the note's
       "Prime Conflict in 3D" a precise form.
       A positive integer is a sum of three squares exactly when it is not of the form 4ᵃ(8b +
       7) (Legendre). Hence no prime p ≡ 7 (mod 8) is the quadrance of a point of ℤ³.

 4.2 Canonical Forms and Unique Factorization
 Two agents that store the same lattice point in frames differing by a unit see different
 coordinates. A canonical associate removes that difference without any shared reference.

       Proposition 4.3 (Canonical Associate). [PROVEN] Address normal form.
       Let q be an integer quaternion with N(q) ≡ 1 (mod 4). Exactly one of its eight
       associates uq, with u ∈ {±1, ±i, ±j, ±k}, has odd positive real part.

       Proof. Squares are 0 or 1 mod 4, so N(q) ≡ 1 forces exactly one odd coordinate. The
       real part of uq runs over ±a, ±b, ±c and ±d as u runs over the eight units, each
       value once. The odd coordinate is non-zero, so it appears with positive sign exactly
       once. ■

 For other norms the canonical associate is defined as the lexicographic maximum of the 24
 Hurwitz associates [DEFINITION]. The unit that carries q to its canonical form is the frame of q
 relative to the standard.

 Scope of the canonical associate. [DEFINITION] It normalizes lattice points used as
 addresses. A quaternion that encodes a rotation must not be replaced by uq, since uq rotates
 space differently. For rotations the Einstein Tile's Form 1 instead uses sign normalization, the
 first non-zero coordinate positive, which leaves x ↦ QxQ̄ /N(Q) unchanged and moves the spinor
 sign to the cycle-step field (Fifteen Lemmas, §6.1). The two normal forms answer different
 questions: which lattice point, and which rotation. Either sign convention for rotations is valid
 once it is stated; the cubit installment makes the same point for the logarithm of a unit
 quaternion, where SLERP implementations replace q by −q when Re q < 0 (Hoard 2026d,
 Proposition 4.6(3)).

 6 Exact Arithmetic and Closure
 Exact Hurwitz arithmetic removes drift from the sieve's lattice evolution outright, and in exact
 arithmetic a periodic schedule of deltas returns to the identity within 12 repetitions or never.

 Status: [PROVEN]. Sources: Fifteen Lemmas, Lemma 13, whose proofs are imported; Hoard
 (2026a), §I.1, for W_Σ = 60, source only.

       Theorem 6.1 (Exact Deltas Close Within 12 Repetitions or Never). [PROVEN]
       Fifteen Lemmas, Lemma 13 Parts 1 and 2; the corollary for schedules is new.
       Let w be a product of rational unit quaternions. If w has finite order, that order is 1, 2,
       3, 4 or 6, so w¹² = 1. Otherwise no power of w is 1; for example the powers of (3 +
       4i)/5 have exact denominators 5ⁿ. In particular the elements of order 5, 8 and 10 in
       the binary octahedral and icosahedral groups 2O and 2I are irrational and cannot
       occur in exact arithmetic, and a periodic schedule of rational deltas that returns at all
       returns within 12 repetitions.

       Proof. A finite-order unit quaternion is cos θ + û sin θ with θ a rational multiple of π,
       and its real part cos θ is rational. By Niven's theorem cos θ ∈ {0, ±1/2, ±1}, so the
       order divides 12. For (3 + 4i)/5: 3 + 4i = (2 + i)² in the Gaussian integers, and the
       prime 2 − i never divides a power of 2 + i, so 5 never divides both components of (3
       + 4i)ⁿ. ■

       Proposition 6.2 (Exact Arithmetic Has No Drift). [PROVEN] Uses Fifteen
       Lemmas, Lemma 13 Part 3.
       Integer and Hurwitz quaternions form rings closed under multiplication, and N(pq) =
       N(p)N(q) exactly. A lattice evolution computed in integer or half-integer arithmetic
       accumulates no rounding error, and the sieve needs no periodic reset. A product of k
       integer quaternions with coordinates below 2^b has coordinates below
       2^{k(b+2)−2}, so exactness lasts as long as the word width allows; Section 7.2
       gives a much sharper bound for the norm-5 generators.

       Proof. Closure and the multiplicativity of the norm are ring identities. Each coordinate
       of a product AB is a signed sum of four products of coordinates, so its largest
       coordinate is at most 4 times the product of the largest coordinates of A and B;
       induction over k factors gives the bound. ■

 Rational, rather than integer, evolution does drift in width: by Theorem 6.1 its denominators
 grow without bound unless the schedule has finite order. Floating-point evolutions still need a
 reset; the right one is a renormalization or a snap to the nearest lattice point, at any interval.

 7.1 The Cubic Lattice as an Abelian Shadow
       Definition 7.1 (The Norm-5 Generators). [DEFINITION]
       S₅ = {1 + 2i, 1 − 2i, 1 + 2j, 1 − 2j, 1 + 2k, 1 − 2k}. Each element has norm 5, and

       Lubotzky, Phillips and Sarnak (1988); [COMPUTED] for L ≤ 5.
       (1) Modulo scalars, S₅ generates a free group of rank 3, whose Cayley graph is the 6-

       division: exactly one g in S₅ leaves qḡ divisible by 5.

 Two results of the Fifteen Lemmas place Theorem 7.2. First, freeness begins with each letter: 1
 + 2i is a Gaussian prime not associate to its conjugate, so by the argument of Fifteen Lemmas,
 Lemma 13 Part 1, no power of it is real, and no one-letter word ever closes [PROVEN]. Second,
 generic tuples of unit quaternions generate free groups (Epstein 1971; Fifteen Lemmas,

 exactly computable. The contrast with units is sharp. A product of Hurwitz units forgets its
 factors: the 13,824 three-step chains of units give only 24 products, 576 chains each, so no
 scheme rebuilds a unit chain's history from its product (Fifteen Lemmas, §7.2). A product of
 norm-5 generators remembers its factors, because each step multiplies the norm by a prime.

 7.2 Generation by Shift and Add
       Proposition 7.3 (Generator Steps Need No Multiplier). [PROVEN] Answers
       BioChain AI (2026d), §8, horizon 2, for S₅.
       For u ∈ {±i, ±j, ±k}, (1 + 2u)q = q + 2(uq) and q(1 + 2u) = q + 2(qu). Multiplying

       step is therefore one signed permutation, one left shift by one bit and one four-lane
       addition.

       Proof. Distributivity gives the first identities. Left multiplication by i sends a + bi + cj
       + dk to −b + ai − dj + ck, and j and k act likewise. ■

 signed byte therefore holds every address to depth 6, 18,750 of them at depth 6, and a 64-bit

 7.3 Why Coordinates Cannot Align Hyperbolic Agents
 Table 9 states the interoperability problem exactly. Two agents that generate content in the

 distance d has coordinates of size about e^d/2, so a fixed number of significant bits resolves

 both failures: the address is the quaternion, and by Theorem 7.2 the quaternion determines its

       pruned values are then recovered by linear interpolation. (3) Q(A, C) + Q(B, C) =

 The midpoint test "2Q₃" of the same stage is the special case Q(A₁, A₃) = 4Q(A₁, A₂) of a

 The TQF also limits where prime traps can sit along a flow line.

       Theorem 8.4 (No Collinear Prime Triple). [PROVEN] Formalizes source §4; a
       second, lattice proof is Fifteen Lemmas, Lemma 5 Part 5.
       Let three points have integer pairwise quadrances Q₁, Q₂, Q₃ satisfying the TQF (Q₁ +
       Q₂ + Q₃)² = 2(Q₁² + Q₂² + Q₃²). If Q₁ and Q₂ are prime, Q₃ is not. In particular, no
       collinear triple of lattice points has three prime quadrances.

       Proof. The TQF holds exactly when √Q₃ = |√Q₁ ± √Q₂|, so Q₃ = Q₁ + Q₂ ± 2√(Q₁Q₂).
       Since Q₃ is an integer, Q₁Q₂ is a perfect square; for primes this forces Q₁ = Q₂ = p,
       and then Q₃ is 0 or 4p, neither of them prime. ■

 Lattice proof. If A, B, C lie on a lattice line, write B − A = sv and C − A = tv with v primitive of
 norm N and s, t distinct non-zero integers. The quadrances are s²N, t²N and (s − t)²N. The first
 two are prime only if s² = t² = 1 and N = p is prime; then t = −s and (s − t)²N = 4p. ■

 Whether a single lattice line nevertheless meets infinitely many nodes of prime norm is a case
 of Bunyakovsky's conjecture (companion, Problem 2); the companion also records how the
 source note's example triple 2, 3, 5 is a right triangle rather than a collinear one (Hoard 2026h,
 Section 3).

 9.3 Non-Local Alignment by Invariants
 Non-local alignment compares agents that share no neighbourhood and no frame, so it can use
 only quantities every frame computes alike.

       Proposition 9.1 (Frame-Free Invariants and the Corrected Coherence).
       [PROVEN] Gives the coherence of BioChain AI (2026h) its sign-invariant form.
       Let u and v be unit quaternions, and let a frame change act on every voxel state by q
       ↦ uqv. (1) It preserves every norm, every pairwise quadrance, the TQF and the
       quadrea A_R. (2) The coherence Φ = (1/N)‖Σ q̂ _i‖ of the voxel-alignment review is
       invariant under it, but not under q_i ↦ −q_i, which represents the same rotation. (3)
       The largest eigenvalue Φ_M of M = (1/N) Σ q̂ _i q̂ _iᵀ is invariant under both, lies
       between 1/4 and 1, and equals 1 exactly when all q̂ _i agree up to sign.

       Proof. (1) The map is linear with |uqv| = |q|, so it is an isometry of ℝ⁴, and every
       listed quantity is built from distances. (2) Σ uq̂ _i v = u(Σ q̂ _i)v has the same length;
       replacing one q̂ _i by its negative changes the sum. (3) M becomes RMRᵀ for the
       orthogonal matrix R of the frame change, and q̂ q̂ ᵀ is unchanged by sign; M has trace
       1, so its largest of four eigenvalues is at least 1/4, and it equals 1 exactly when M has
       rank one. ■

 Remark (Measured). [COMPUTED] For 64 voxel states near a common pole, Φ = 0.9969
 before and after a random frame change and 0.0331 after random sign flips; Φ_M = 0.9938 in
 all three cases. Matching anchors between two frames by their sorted quadrances to all other
 anchors, a frame-free signature, is correct in 499 of 500 trials (Appendix A, sections S and T).

 The sign correction is not cosmetic. At the Engine's two handshake windows, steps 2 and 6 of
 the cycle, the spinors are i and −i: the same half-turn with opposite spin sign, so a partner sees
 the same spatial orientation, and the spin sign is the one bit the geometry never exposes
 (Fifteen Lemmas, Theorem 7.5). A coherence score that changes when q becomes −q
 measures that hidden bit rather than alignment; Φ_M does not.

 Scale-normalized quads, as in the folded-packet paper, make the signatures invariant under a
 change of unit as well (BioChain AI 2026i, §9), so two agents can match anchors before they
 agree on a length. The cubit supplies the physical counterpart.

 Remark (Medium-Free Invariants). [PROVEN] Hoard (2026d), Lemma 3.2. If an enclosure
 measures N_x, N_y and N_z cubits, each realized in the air it contains, its mode frequencies are
 8 f_cal √(l²/N_x² + m²/N_y² + n²/N_z²), whatever the temperature, pressure and composition of
 that air. Frame-free invariants remove the orientation an agent cannot share; cubit units
 remove the medium. Two agents that exchange lengths as cubit counts rather than in metres
 agree on the phase geometry at f_cal of everything they describe, though their air differs

       For matched point sets, the rotation that minimizes the sum of squared distances is

 Remark (Two Agents, Measured). [COMPUTED] In 500 trials, agent B held agent A's 12

 That group is exact: x ↦ 2ρ_g(x) maps the core cube onto the voxel cube exactly when ρ_g lies

 9.5 One-Way Compatibility and Complementary Encodings
 An external result on quantum codes gives a precise model of partial compatibility
 [ESTABLISHED]. Butt et al. (2026) pair the tetrahedral [[15, 1, 3]] colour code with its
 Hadamard-transformed twin. A transversal CNOT preserves the code space only from the
 tetrahedral code to its twin; the reverse direction needs a pieceable round-robin CZ gate with a
 mid-circuit measurement of the stabilizers that stay invariant throughout. The Eastin–Knill
 theorem forbids a universal transversal gate set in any single code.

 Read as an analogy [HEURISTIC]: two agents whose lattice charts differ by a global transform
 can translate cheaply in a direction whose checks are invariants of that transform; the other
 direction needs checks interleaved during translation, on the invariant set of Section 9.3, which
 here consists of norms, canonical words and images mod q. No single chart makes every
 operation cheap for every agent, so the standard fixes the invariants, not one privileged frame.


 9.6 Consensus, Governance and the Limits of a Coherence Score
 On the meeting graph X(5, q) any diffusive consensus, such as gossip averaging, converges in

 The chiral engine paper splits governance between a fluid Helix A, the BioStrata Open Plane,
 and a crystalline Helix B, the HyperSim Genesis Chain, with Proof-of-Manifold verification by the
 TQF (BioChain AI 2026f, §4). The Dual-Helix principle fixes what each side may do. A certificate
 can establish that a packet is well formed, that its claims are consistent across frames, and
 that it was derived by stated operations from stated inputs. It cannot establish that its content

 11 Conclusion
 The Fokker-Planck prime sieve is the Helix A half of a Dual-Helix system: a geometric process
 that proposes where the primes are, paired with exact arithmetic that certifies them. Its
 mathematics is exact, and its use for communication between agents is stated as a design.

 What the sieve is. On a lattice the sieve is a master equation. Its Gibbs state is positive on
 every node of a finite lattice, and on the infinite Hurwitz lattice exactly when the Hurwitz
 partition function converges; absorbing traps send all mass to the prime set with the harmonic
 measure; killing concentrates without selecting; and drift without a potential carries a current
 and produces entropy but leaves the stationary state uniform (Propositions 2.2 to 2.6). By
 Kolmogorov's criterion a potential selects at no stationary cost while a pump circulates without
 selecting. Certified traps are exact whatever the dynamics, and the Engine's wheel gives the
 proposer a proven bias of 3.75, the largest of any primorial wheel that fits the packet's 3-bit
 field (Propositions 3.1 to 3.3). The sieve lives in four dimensions, where every prime splits,
 every lattice point has a canonical associate and primitive points factor uniquely in a fixed
 norm order. The golden Clifford torus holds no lattice point, and its Fibonacci approximants
 carry the points q_m whose prime norms 7, 47 and 2207 are invisible to three dimensions
 (Theorems 5.2 and 5.3). Exact arithmetic has no drift, and exact delta schedules close within
 12 repetitions or never (Section 6).

 What it enables. The norm-5 quaternions 1 ± 2i, 1 ± 2j, 1 ± 2k grow a hyperbolic tree over
 the cubic lattice by shifts and adds alone, with unique addresses and Ramanujan quotients on
 which diffusion mixes in logarithmic time, so procedurally generated cubic integers can be
 exchanged as exact words rather than as coordinates in a frame neither agent can see (Section
 7). On the Einstein Tile the sieve runs in exact 64-bit arithmetic, certifies anchor packets with a
 2,017-byte table, keys the Codex on canonical forms and prunes streams losslessly (Section 8).
 Anchored to the realized cubit, the lattice has a physical scale, a precision ceiling near Q ≈ 2.3
 × 10⁷ under which the Triple Quad Formula stays exact in 64-bit integers, and invariants that
 hold in any air. These define a universal lattice standard on which agents with no shared frame,
 medium or history align by invariants, solve their relative frame by Horn's quaternion, snap it
 to the cube group the Engine's own tier maps generate, and record every certificate under a
 Merkle root (Section 9).

 Where the rest is. Every claim of the source note and of the companion papers that this
 paper corrects or replaces, the status of every claim, the open problems with their test
 protocols, and the relation to earlier versions of this installment are collected in the companion
 (Hoard 2026h).
