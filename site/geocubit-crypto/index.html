03 / GEOMETRIC ENGINE OF TIME

An intrinsic logical clock—not a substitute for freshness.

The zero-point eight-step spinor cycle and threefold offset produce a 24-frame schedule. A monotonic epoch and message counters keep that cycle from repeating into an accepted replay.

INTERACTIVE / EXACT MODULAR ARITHMETIC

Advance the frame

Spinor phase0 / 8
Threefold phase0 / 3
24-frame epoch0

A cyclic phase repeats. The full anti-replay identity also contains a non-wrapping session epoch and sequence number.

Why 8 × 3?

phase(n) = (n mod 8, n mod 3)joint period = lcm(8, 3) = 24

Eight spinor steps bring the idealized phase back to its starting value. Three independent 120° triads describe a second cyclic index. The combined finite schedule can organize deterministic computation and frame selection.

Security boundary: this phase is predictable and periodic. It is neither a nonce nor a key. A physical clock has additional offset, drift, propagation-delay and uncertainty terms.

The Engine's conditional traversal results apply to specified procedurally integrable families; they do not prove arbitrary P = NP or generic O(1) computation.

05 / CANONICAL GEOMETRIC HASHES

Hash the geometry. Don't confuse it with a key.

Canonicalization is the essential bridge: two nodes must agree on the exact bytes representing a point. A secure hash then commits to that representation. Geometry alone supplies no preimage hardness.

INTERACTIVE / UNKEYED SHA-256 DEMO

Transcript-binding laboratory

Demo encodes each UTF-8 field with a 32-bit big-endian length. Browser SHA-256 is used only for pedagogy; the proposed authenticated profile uses canonical binary tuples with SHA3/TupleHash/KMAC.

Public commitment vs. keyed authentication

H = SHA3-256(encode(G, message))

Anyone can recompute a public content commitment. It detects modification relative to an authenticated expected value, but cannot prove who sent it.

tag = KMAC256(K, encode(G, message), 256, "GCH-v1")

A keyed MAC authenticates under an established secret. The geometric context remains public associated data, not a source of secret entropy.

Fixed-point quantization, normalization of equivalent quaternions, field lengths, integer overflow and cross-platform representations require explicit negative tests.

06 / THREE RESEARCH FRONTIERS

Interesting geometry. Different security claims.

These mechanisms can be evaluated separately. None should silently become a confidentiality or authentication primitive.

OPEN / HARDNESS
H³

Hyperbolic cryptography

Use hyperbolic group paths for compact hierarchy and routing. Quaternionic delta chains preserve order; they are generally invertible, so noncommutativity is not a trapdoor.

Test: address collisions, exact canonical forms, group attacks and quantum algorithms.
HEURISTIC / COST
∮

Forced simulation

Commit to sequential geometric state transitions as a possible work-cost or anti-spam mechanism. Fast-forward, precomputation and parallel shortcuts must be investigated.

Test: work-depth lower bounds, CPU/GPU baselines and quantum speedups.
EXPERIMENTAL / CHANNEL
∿

Longitudinal waves

Acoustic carriers may embed authenticated symbols, calibration beacons or auxiliary channel measurements; they remain observable, spoofable, replayable and jammable.

Test: BER, SNR, replay, multipath and eavesdropper location.
ETH

What the 2026 ETH research changes—and what it doesn't.

OpenAI published a subexponential SAT/#SAT algorithm claim and accompanying formalization that, if its stated scope and proof checks stand, would refute the classical Exponential Time Hypothesis. Community review and practical constants matter. Refuting ETH does not prove P = NP, break ML-KEM, or establish hardness for a geometric puzzle. The Geometric Engine's conditional traversal theorem is a separate, narrower claim.

07 / SECURITY AUDIT

Design for the adversary, not the diagram.

Choose a threat to see which controls are necessary. The absence of an attack in a geometric model is not a cryptographic proof.

QUANTUM ADVERSARY

Harvest-now, decrypt-later

Recorded ciphertext may be attacked by a future quantum computer. Classical RSA/ECDH-only handshakes are insufficient for long-lived secrecy.

Minimum control

Deploy authenticated post-quantum key establishment with suitable forward secrecy and long-term credential rotation.

Residual limitation

A post-quantum algorithm does not eliminate endpoint compromise, bad randomness or side-channel attacks.
